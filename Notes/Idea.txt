That gets overwhelming fast. To stand out, your team can test and compare algorithmic feedback modes:Velocity-Sensitive Warning Mode: Vibration activates only when the rate of closure (approach speed) toward an obstacle exceeds a safety threshold.Edge & Silhouette Detection: The algorithm compares neighboring sensors in the $3\times3$ grid to vibrate only along the outer contour of an obstacle, helping the user feel its shape and opening.Logarithmic Tactile Scaling: Human skin senses changes in vibration non-linearly. Programming a logarithmic intensity curve matches human perception far better than a linear curve.
