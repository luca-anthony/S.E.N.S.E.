# Science-Fair-SHLS

This is a science fair project for three students at SHLS.

Our goal is to make home automation cheaper and easier for people to use.\
Whether that is from just buying something to control lights or something to control advanced tasks such as 3D printing.\
This is not our only goal, however; we also wish to bring wonder to the people who may use this.

This has been something we have been thinking about for a long time and now have an opportunity to bring it to life.

This is ENLIGHTEN. Bringing light to the world.
