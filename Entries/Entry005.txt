How can we present? How do we present? 

Idea #1: Tri-fold poster
  If we use a tri-fold poster then we can present our information in an environmentally safe way. 
We can also change out certain things on the poster with ease. 

Idea #2: Laminated poster board. 
  We can use a very big, laminated to look more clean and formal. 
We can also jam pack more information in it. 

Cons:
  *The Trifold poster can't have as much information as the Laminated poster.
  *The Laminated poster has the problem of build volume.
  *The Trifold poster isn't as formal.
  *The Laminated poster is more expensive. 

Conclusion
  My conclusion is that we use a laminated poster as it is more informational and formal.
However for the video summary, we use a Slideshow. 
I say this because we can make it better on a slideshow by evenly presenting information. 
But for contest or anything else, we use a poster on a stand. Preferably with the glove and a computer on the side. 
The abstract also needs to be given its own sheet protector and put on a display stand.
